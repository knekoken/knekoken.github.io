Structural Control Laboratory(Kaneko Lab)

Department of Architecture, Osaka Metropolitan University

How do buildings shake in earthquakes, and how can we protect people and cities? We address it through control theory, monitoring, shake table tests, simulation and digital twins.

Current student projects (2026)

4th-year undergraduates

  • Development of a tuned mass damper (TMD) for high-rise urban timber moment frames
  • A field trial toward the wider use of low-cost structural monitoring systems with AI
  • Creating new architectural spaces from combinations of diverse dampers
  • Indoor disaster preparedness from the viewpoint of architectural sensibility in the age of free-address offices

Master's and doctoral students

  • A systematic design scheme for coupled vibration-controlled buildings (high-rise condominiums) under large-amplitude ground motions
  • Theory and experiments on vibration-controlled structures for flexible, adaptable spatial structures inspired by living organisms
  • Experiments and simulation of human injuries from furniture overturning in earthquakes
  • Realizing diverse vibration control systems with inerter dampers
  • Rapid disaster response systems using AI and multimodal data
  • New structural optimization that accounts for post-earthquake downtime
  • Development of low-cost tuned mass dampers (TMDs) for low- and mid-rise buildings
  • A digital-twin damage assessment system linked with mixed reality and structural monitoring
